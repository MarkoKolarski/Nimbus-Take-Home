# Master Service Agreement

**Between:** Alice Nimbus Corp ("Provider") and Acme Widgets Ltd ("Client")
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-15

## 1. Scope

Provider grants Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the
WidgetFlow platform for internal inventory management purposes. This
agreement covers hosting, support, and quarterly feature updates for the
WidgetFlow platform.

## 2. Term

This agreement runs for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months from the
Effective Date, renewing automatically for successive twelve (12) month
periods unless either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 3. Fees

Client shall pay Provider a quarterly license fee of $18,500, invoiced net
30. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

## 4. Support

Provider commits to a 4-hour response time for Severity 1 incidents
affecting the WidgetFlow production environment, and 24 hours for all other
requests, during business hours (09:00-18:00 CET, Monday-Friday).

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep the other's confidential information secret for
five (5) years after termination of this agreement.
