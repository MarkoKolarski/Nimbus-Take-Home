MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between
Alice Nimbus Corp and Beacon Analytics Inc for the purpose of evaluating a
potential data-sharing partnership around the ClearSight reporting
dashboard.

Each party agrees not to disclose the other's proprietary business
information, source code, or customer data disclosed during the evaluation
period, which runs for six (6) months from the date of signature.

This Agreement does not obligate either party to enter into any further
business relationship. Either party may terminate this Agreement with
thirty (30) days' written notice.

Governing law: State of Delaware.
