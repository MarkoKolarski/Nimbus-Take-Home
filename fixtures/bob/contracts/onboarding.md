# Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Bob Harbor Logistics! This guide covers what to expect in your
first two weeks.

## Week 1: Setup

- IT will issue a laptop and badge on your first day. VPN access is
  provisioned through the SecureLink client; credentials arrive by separate
  email from it@bobharborlogistics.example.
- Your manager will schedule a 1:1 within the first three days to walk
  through your onboarding checklist in the HR portal.
- Complete the mandatory safety training module before visiting the
  warehouse floor.

## Week 2: Systems

- Access to the FleetTrack dispatch system is granted after the safety
  training is marked complete.
- Payroll runs on the 15th and last day of each month; direct deposit forms
  are due by the 5th.
