EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - BOB HARBOR LOGISTICS

Working hours: 08:00-16:30, Monday through Friday, with a 30-minute unpaid
lunch break.

Time off: Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off per year,
accrued monthly. Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 5 days into the
following year.

Warehouse safety: Steel-toed boots and high-visibility vests are mandatory
on the warehouse floor at all times. Forklift operation requires a current
certification on file with the safety office.

Remote work: Office staff may work remotely up to two days per week with
manager approval; warehouse and dispatch roles are on-site only.

Code of conduct violations should be reported to
hr@bobharborlogistics.example or through the anonymous ethics hotline
posted in the break room.
